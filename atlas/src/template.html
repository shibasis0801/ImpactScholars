      How to use this atlas

      • Read across the spread, then turn with ← →, the space bar, the mouse wheel or the strip under the pages.
      • Dotted words are glossary terms, underlined names are citations, and section numbers link across the guide. Hover to preview; click to open them in the companion pane.
      • The companion pane follows your reading and shows the explorer for the section you are on. Pin it to keep one open while you read on.
      • Click a diagram to open it large in the Read tab, or put any section there to read two places side by side.
      • Videos and papers open in a new tab here. In the local copy they play inside the pane.
